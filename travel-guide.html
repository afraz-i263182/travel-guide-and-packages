PREPARE FOR THE JOURNEY
BEFORE YOU SET OFF

Plan your trip with confidence

A few simple preparations can make a big difference to your travel experience.

◷

Choose your season

Consider the kind of experience you want. Spring brings fresh colours, summer is popular for outdoor travel, autumn offers changing foliage, and winter requires extra preparation for cold and snow.

⌖

Plan your route

Check the route, expected travel time and road conditions before departure. Mountain journeys can take longer than expected, so keep your schedule flexible.

☼

Check the weather

Mountain weather can change quickly. Review a reliable forecast close to your travel date and follow local advisories about road or trail conditions.

QUICK ANSWERS

Travel questions

What should I check before travelling to a mountain destination?

Check the weather forecast, road and route updates, local guidance, accommodation confirmation and any travel requirements for your destination.

Should I pack warm clothes in summer?

It is sensible to pack at least one warm layer. Temperatures can vary with altitude, time of day and weather conditions.

How can I travel more responsibly?

Respect local communities and customs, avoid littering, use marked routes where appropriate, and leave natural places as you found them.
